Scores contributing to grade

Redo 1 can improve one score in Modules 1–7. Redo 2 and Module 15 / Redo 3 can improve up to two different scores in Modules 8–14. Only improvements count. The lowest remaining score is then dropped if all 14 required modules are complete and Module 14 is at least 10/15. Module 15 / Redo 3 is optional.

Canvas cannot automatically account for redo quizzes and the dropped score, so its percentage may differ from this calculation.
